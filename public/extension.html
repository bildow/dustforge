Before you start

  1. Install the DemiPass Android app and sign in. Your phone approves every pairing and every fill.
  2. Add the extension, open it and pair it from the app.
  3. Sign in to a site with a password you type, then tap Save.
  4. Next time, choose the login and approve the fill with your PIN on your phone.

Installs only through your browser's own add-on prompt or store. DemiPass never asks you to run a script, change a browser policy or edit the registry.

What the extension sends and stores: demipass.com/privacy. Help: support@dustforge.com.
